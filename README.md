# 💍 Abdul Razique Chauhan & Alisha Naz — Royal Islamic Wedding Website

An ultra-luxurious, interactive 3D wedding invitation and celebration website recreated with authentic royal Islamic aesthetics, inspired by the high-end Claude artifact design.

---

## 🌟 Final Key Features

1. **3D Opening Velvet Invitation Book**:
   - Royal Emerald Green velvet book cover with gold foil filigree.
   - Star and Crescent Islamic medallion (*Rub el Hizb*) with "NIKAH MUBARAK" emblem.
   - Physics-based parallax tilt on mouse/touch move.
   - Smooth book cover opening animation leading into the royal palace arch.
   - Touch-optimized with instant opening support.

2. **Custom Acoustic Sufi Background Music**:
   - High-fidelity acoustic Sufi wedding music (`audio/background_music.mp3`).
   - Interactive floating audio equalizer button with toggle play/pause.
   - Built-in live Web Audio fallback ensemble.

3. **Falling Champagne Gold & Jasmine Petals**:
   - HTML5 canvas particle physics with 3D rotation, wobble, and gentle wind drift.

4. **Royal Hero Page with Character Typewriter Reveal**:
   - Holy Quranic *Bismillahir Rahmanir Raheem* calligraphy.
   - Character-by-character typewriter reveal for **Abdul Razique Chauhan** & **Alisha Naz**.
   - Bold, compact family lineage (Son of Mohd. Bilal Chauhan, Granddaughter of Marhoom Ismail Ji Banthia, etc.).
   - Palace architecture background (`photos/p3.jpg`).

5. **Silk Tassel Drag-to-Reveal Scroll & Live Countdown**:
   - Interactive gold-and-emerald tassel that users can pull down to roll out the royal parchment scroll.
   - Real-time countdown timer tracking Days, Hours, Minutes, and Seconds until Nikah (17 Rajab 1448 Hijri / 27 Dec 2026).

6. **Wedding Events Carousel**:
   - **Rawaangi-e-Barat** (Sunday, 27 Dec 2026, 8:00 AM · Malkapur to Timarni)
   - **Mubarak Nikah** (Sunday, 27 Dec 2026, Baad Namaz-e-Maghrib · Timarni)
   - **Dawat-e-Walima** (Tuesday, 29 Dec 2026, Baad Namaz-e-Maghrib · Bharat Lawns, Malkapur)
   - 3D curved cylinder perspective with swipe gesture support.

7. **Dastaan-e-Ishq (Sacred Union Gallery)**:
   - 3D interactive story carousel showcasing 5 artistic couple moments (`photos/story_1.jpg` to `photos/story_5.jpg`).
   - Islamic Ayah "And We created you in pairs" (*Wakhalaqnakum Azwaja* - Surah An-Naba 78:8).
   - Touch-enabled 3D card cylinder with swipe gestures and navigation dots.

8. **Grand Dawat-e-Walima Velvet Card**:
   - Deep emerald velvet card with twinkling gold stars and royal feast details.

9. **Venue Map Card & One-Click Contact Calling**:

## 🚀 How to Run & View

1. **Local Browser**:
   - Simply double-click [`index.html`](index.html) to open it in Chrome, Edge, Safari, or Firefox.
   - The site is 100% self-contained and mobile-responsive.

2. **Live Hosting (GitHub Pages / Netlify / Vercel)**:
   - Upload the repository folder to GitHub and enable **GitHub Pages** under Settings > Pages.
   - Or drag-and-drop the folder directly onto [Netlify Drop](https://app.netlify.com/drop) for an instant live URL to share with family and friends on WhatsApp.
